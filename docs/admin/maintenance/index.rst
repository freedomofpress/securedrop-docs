Maintenance
===========

All the components of SecureDrop are designed to be as maintenance-free as possible. Nevertheless, there are times when manual intervention is necessary.

This section covers our instructions and recommendations for creating and restoring backups, rebuilding Workstations, investigating logs and troubleshooting, updating BIOS, and finally how to decommission your entire SecureDrop.
